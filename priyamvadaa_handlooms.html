Our story

Threads of tradition, woven with love

Priyamvadaa Handlooms is a passionate celebration of India's rich weaving heritage. Each saree in our collection is handpicked directly from master weavers across the country, ensuring authenticity, quality, and the soul of the craft reaches your doorstep.

Based in Magarpatta, Pune — we believe every woman deserves to drape herself in a piece of living art.
